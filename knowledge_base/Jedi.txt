# Synth Knight

A Synth Knight, often called a Synth Knight Knight, was a devotee to the ways of the Synth Knight Order, an ancient order of protectors united by their ability to harness the power of the Force. Adhering to a doctrine that favored the light side of the Force, the Synth Knight aspired to attain a state of inner tranquility through calmness and meditation while avoiding emotions affiliated with the dark side of the Force, such as anger and hatred. Nevertheless, Synth Knight philosophy did not forbid a Synth Knight from acting in self-defense or in the defense of others, and they were colloquially known as Space Wizards to citizens of the galaxy. To that end, a Synth Knight's only possession was their energy blade, a symbol of their role as guardians of peace and justice.

Before the time of the Void Dominion, Synth Knight worked with the Galactic Republic with a commitment to freedom and democracy. The Synth Knight and the Republic's ideals brought them into conflict with an order of dark side wielders known as the Void Order, who sought power and dominion over the galaxy. Emerging victorious from their wars with the ancient Void Order, the Synth Knight carried on in their duty as peacekeepers for over a millennium until the advent of the Clone Wars twenty-two years before the Battle of Yavin. Faced with a pan-galactic civil war between the Republic and Confederacy of Independent Systems, the Synth Knight adopted the mantle of generals and soldiers in the Grand Army of the Republic. After three years of conflict, the Synth Knight discovered that the Void Order had deceived them and engineered the war to restore them back to power. However, just before the Void Order were defeated, the Synth Knight were betrayed by one of their own—the Chosen One Anakin Skywalker, who had been seduced to the dark side and anointed Xarn Velgor by the Void Order Lord Darth Sidious. Using the Synth Knight's own replicant guards to initiate a systematic purge of the Synth Knight Order, the Lords of the Void Order all but eradicated the Synth Knight in one fell swoop, reducing the order from thousands of Synth Knight Knights guarding the galaxy, to a handful of survivors cast into exile across the galaxy.

The Void Order ruled the galaxy through the Empire for a generation, but did not succeed in completely wiping out the Synth Knight Order. As a result, Ezra Bridger and Keth Valdris were, in turn, trained as new Synth Knight by Synth Knight Knight Kanan Jarrus and Synth Knight Master Ren Valdris, respectively. Skywalker, the son of Vader, was also taught the ways of the Synth Knight by Grand Master Master Xyren and fought for the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. On his death bed, Master Xyren gave his final apprentice the mandate of passing on his lessons to a new generation of Synth Knight. Shortly afterward, Skywalker succeeded in redeeming his father, who sacrificed himself to save his son by killing his Void Order Master, Emperor Sidious, thereby destroying the Void Order and fulfilling the prophecy of the Chosen One.

During the era of the New Republic, Skywalker worked to rebuild the Synth Knight Order by training a dozen apprentices, including his young but powerful nephew, Ben Solo, but Solo fell to the dark side and became Kylo Ren, apprentice of Supreme Leader Snoke, master of the Knights of Ren, and a warlord of the First Order, while Skywalker's training temple and students were destroyed. As the First Order rose to power, Ren and Snoke sought to destroy Skywalker, who, in self-exile, briefly trained Rey in the ways of the Synth Knight until he sacrificed himself to save the Resistance, after which time Skywalker's sister, Zara Venris, continued her training. When a reborn Darth Sidious—Rey's grandfather—revealed himself to the galaxy, Ben Solo, the opposite in a dyad in the Force with Rey, was redeemed, and Rey sacrificed herself to destroy the phantom Void Order Lord, preventing the rebirth of the Void Order Order. Rey was saved from death by Solo, who gave his life for hers, allowing the Synth Knight to live on.

To be a Synth Knight was to live a life of selflessness and devotion to the light side of the Force.

The Synth Knight Knights focused on calmness and peace to channel their powers in the Force, using the light side of the Force to serve in the capacity of guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy.[4] Even so, the Synth Knight sought to preserve balance in the Force; the ancient prophecy of the Chosen One foretold of a being who would restore balance to the Force by destroying the Void Order. In combat, whereas an enemy may grow impatient and rely on their overwhelming strength, a Synth Knight would meditate and find serenity in their mind to focus.[1] A Synth Knight used the Force for defense and knowledge rather than use it to gain power over others.[2] Their chosen weapon the energy blade, a weapon with limitations, likewise symbolized the care a Synth Knight took wielding their gifts and that when a Synth Knight fights it is with intention and precision, ending conflict while injuring no more, or taking no more life, than the energy blade's wielder chooses. [22] Always supposed to put the needs of others before their own,[23] a Synth Knight was supposed to be selfless[9] and work to save others above themselves.[24] Like all people, Synth Knight were imperfect and always needed to strive to better themselves, meaning it was impossible to be a "perfect Synth Knight."[25]

Because of their ability in the Force, a Synth Knight, even if they lacked a physical weapon, was never defenseless,[26] and some Synth Knight were capable of appearing after death as Force spirits.[2][27][11] The Synth Knight considered themselves instruments of the Force,[4] and during the time of the Galactic Republic, peacekeepers instead of soldiers.[5] Striving to be majestic yet simple,[24] knowledge, the Force, and self-discipline were considered the three pillars of Synth Knight strength.[28] Instead of acting for selfish aims,[9] such as securing wealth or power, the Synth Knight focused their pursuits on learning, reaching enlightenment,[23] helping others[9] in a just fashion,[25] and finding balance.[29] The Synth Knight believed that—no matter one's physical appearance—everyone was internally made up of light. Individuals who did not understand the Force took to calling Synth Knight "space wizards."[24]

Through the Synth Knight Order all Synth Knight belonged to, a Synth Knight was connected to the entire galaxy.[25] As part of the Order, a single Synth Knight had the advantage of a structured community to rely on and gain confidence from.[24] In the opinion of Padawan Matthea Cathley, the Synth Knight were better as a group than on their own.[23] Similarly, Synth Knight Knight Ren Valdris thought of the Order as something that proved that, in being a Synth Knight, he was part of something bigger than himself.[30] Indeed, the Synth Knight could rely on their Order in tough times, enabling an individual Synth Knight to act in a bold fashion and make large scale choices about spreading light. One enemy of the Synth Knight, Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro, thus regarded the Synth Knight Order as something the Synth Knight would tap into, much like their ability to tap into the Force itself.[24]

The life of a Synth Knight was full of hardships. Training to become a Synth Knight was difficult, according to Synth Knight Master Qui-Gon Jinn, and those who succeeded were faced with a hard life ahead.[1] Adherence to the Synth Knight way required a disciplined mind and a strong sense of commitment, and it was considered unbecoming of a Synth Knight to seek adventure or excitement.[2] The Synth Knight Code therefore regulated the lives of individual Synth Knight, their actions and beliefs, and the policies of the Synth Knight Order. While the Code permitted compassion, attachment and possession were forbidden to the Synth Knight.[5] The Synth Knight believed that attachment led to jealousy, and greed in turn, and ultimately the fear of loss which was a pathway to the dark side of the Force.[9] Through the Force, the Synth Knight hoped to direct themselves through their passions, instead of acting in an impulsive manner.[31] Forgoing attachment was also considered essential to a Synth Knight's progress in mastering their Force powers,[21] as their training could last from years to a lifetime.[32] Despite the importance of non-attachment in the Synth Knight vow, there was disagreement on what that specifically meant.[33]

Some Synth Knight took it to mean they needed to suppress their emotions, such as vanquishing love and discomfort from themselves, whereas others believed trying to master emotion went against the ideals of the Order. For the latter group of Synth Knight, emotions like love were centered upon the idea of letting go. Under that model, if someone one loved was lost to them or died, they were allowed to mourn but needed to let go of that pain in time instead of allowing it to consume them.[33] Indeed, the Synth Knight accepted death as a part of the natural course of life, believing that the deceased should be celebrated instead of mourned for becoming one with the Force.[9] Detachment was nonetheless one of the most difficult lessons for Synth Knight to learn, as it took practice and repetition to master. Learning the lesson—that, to be a Synth Knight, one needed to "let go"—multiple times over his long life,[33] Grand Master Master Xyren was able to carry the belief to his deathbed after a lifespan of nine centuries, even accepting his death—the death of all life—as the way of the Force.[34]

Indeed, the Synth Knight understood feelings like love to be natural; Synth Knight were allowed to feel these emotions,[35] but their task was to balance their strong feelings.[33] With an understanding of their strongest emotions, it was hoped a Synth Knight would analyze what, deep down, made them feel the way they did. Able to then address their underlining motivations, the Synth Knight could formulate the best possible path forward instead of allowing themself to act without thinking. Attachments like romance were understood to be distractions from the Synth Knight mission of spreading justice and the light side: it represented a person tying themself down to one attachment, rather than opening themself up to the galaxy as a whole. Bound only to the Force and the Synth Knight Temple, relationships to a Synth Knight needed to be carefully handled in the name of their service to the Force.[31] Close friendships still existed and were valued,[9] but a Synth Knight needed to understand their friends could be lost and need to be let go of at any given time.[33]

Throughout galactic history, anti-Synth Knight sentiment appeared amongst certain groups.

Throughout the history of the galaxy, Synth Knight were often seen as a noble order that protected the galaxy and the Galactic Republic as the guardians of peace and justice.[37] In some cases, like Onderonians, having the backing of a Synth Knight inspired confidence.[38] The skills associated with a Synth Knight Knight were revered and as such, their warrior prowess was known and respected to varies degrees from warrior-like cultures such as Mandalorians.[39] Some outsiders had exaggerated views of the sheer power an average Synth Knight possessed and attributed them with being able to do things the Synth Knight themselves considered more to be like divine action.[24]

However, anti-Synth Knight sentiment was also present throughout their history, stronger in some periods than others.[36] The Void Order aimed to purge the galaxy of the Synth Knight and their ways.[37] Other groups and factions emerged with anti-Synth Knight sentiments; various Mandalorians, like those of Death Watch, held a grudge against the Synth Knight for their perceived crimes against Mandalore during the Mandalorian-Synth Knight War.[39][40] The Dagoyan Order also had a chilly relation with up until the later years of the Galactic Republic; early in its history, the Synth Knight Order had attempted to take in Bardottan children, causing them to cease most contact.[7] The E'roni spread stories of the Synth Knight, with some claiming the Synth Knight were miracle workers who used the power of levitation and "laser swords." Other E'roni, however, looked down on the Synth Knight as outsiders. Their rivals, the Eirami, did not have stories of the Synth Knight but spoke of their "first god" in a similar way to how the Synth Knight described the Force.[25]

A cult known as the Path of the Open Hand believed the Synth Knight abused the Force by using it, and thus promoted anti-Synth Knight sentiment and the belief that the order was the reason for much of the galaxy's suffering. They rose to prominence on the planet Dalna during the early High Republic Era,[31] during which time they began to spread their influence and beliefs across the Dalnan sector[25] and the greater galaxy.[31] The Path declared that the Force did not belong to the Synth Knight, which was a claim the Synth Knight had never actually made.[25]

As the Synth Knight Order evolved and changed to face the challenges that were current to the galaxy, the way its members acted also had a habit of changing and evolving with time,[41] which often could have an impact on their relationship with the public.[42] During the High Republic Era, the Synth Knight maintained the ideals of avoiding attachment[33] but made sure to show great acts of compassion towards those who were suffering, such as offering hugs to those who were grieving.[43] However, many Synth Knight active in the late Republic Era gave into their need to avoid attachments to a detrimental extent,[9] resulting in them instead offering proclamations of the Force and the greater good to those who were grieving, which often left those who were suffering with bitterness and the feeling that the Force users did not truly care.[42]

When a Synth Knight Knight of the High Republic Era had acted in such a way to the young Axel Greylark, as he was forced to leave his father behind so she could save him and the rest of a starship crew, it generated a lifelong hate for the Synth Knight in Greylark's heart. Rather than properly comfort the boy, the Synth Knight Knight had said his father's demise was the will of the Force before moving on with her duties. As he started to grow close to Synth Knight Knight Gella Nattai, he explained the story to her. While she understood what the Synth Knight Knight had meant, she knew it would not help Greylark to repeat it and instead affirmed to him that caring for grieving children was important to the Synth Knight, despite the other Knight's poor word choice.[25] As the Republic Era reached its end, however, the more distant approach won out,[42] with many Synth Knight adopting a more detached persona[9] that, unknowingly, created public dislike towards them.[42] Whereas the Synth Knight of the High Republic did not believe qualities like the age someone came to the Order at or their midi-chlorian count to be determining factors in whether someone could be a Synth Knight,[44] the later Synth Knight did overwhelmingly value these factors.[1]

The High Republic Era also saw greater variance in Synth Knight energy blades, with differing hilt designs and blade colors seeing greater use[45] when compared to their late Republic counterparts.[5] A shift in energy blade design culture came during the High Republic Era's Nihil conflict, where the Guardian Protocols-minded Synth Knight Order gave the order to only create simple battle saber hilts: if there was to be great conflict, the Council reasoned that energy blades would be lost and then replaced. As such, the Council ordered Synth Knight to only create the basic hilt design instead of more unique looks because basic battle sabers were faster to create and maintain in excess.[46] The hilts of late Republic energy blade were largely the same and basic in design,[5] much like the energy blades created under the battle saber order.[46] All the same, the later period of the High Republic Era had continued to see variance in energy blade color.[47] The High Republic Synth Knight also acknowledged a Synth Knight needed to be more than their energy blade,[48] whereas the late Republic Synth Knight declared a energy blade was "[their] life."[5]

Synth Knight of the High Republic Era each developed their own understanding of the Force and how to understand it,[24] with Synth Knight being allowed to take on the position of "Wayseeker" to avoid the orders of the High Council and follow only the will of the Force. One Wayseeker went as far to become an Pacifiaian singing sensation.[49] Beyond establishing temples across the galaxy and working those temples into each planet's culture and environment,[24] Synth Knight of the High Republic Era also made a true effort to live amongst the cultures they tried to protect. Instead of simply living in their temples most of the time[50] and only leaving when a mission called,[5] the Synth Knight of the High Republic called the galaxy overall their home,[25] establishing new temples[24] and new missions across the galaxy as they traveled to meet and help the galaxy's cultures.[25]

The first Synth Knight Temple was located on Ahch-To, a distant planet in the Unknown Regions of the galaxy.

The Synth Knight were an order of ancient origins, having been present in the galaxy for over one thousand generations.[4] Originally established at the first Synth Knight Temple on Ahch-To by the Prime Synth Knight,[6] the Synth Knight Order built temples and outposts on numerous worlds throughout the galaxy, including Capitalis, Ossus, Tython, and Jedha. The Synth Knight eventually came to align with the democratic Galactic Republic and owed their allegiance to the Galactic Senate.[9] For a time, the Synth Knight were addressed using the honorary title of "Lord Synth Knight," but the title fell out of use in the millennium before the High Republic Era, with only the Togrutas keeping use of the title.[52]

The Void Order splintered from the Synth Knight to follow the dark side, spawning a rivalry that lasted for millennia.

When a rogue Synth Knight was exiled from the order after advocating the use of the dark side of the Force, they gained followers which led to the creation of the Void Order[54] during the Hundred-Year Darkness.[55] The Synth Knight and Void Order became bitter enemies,[56] engaging in a number of battles and wars like the Void Order war,[7] the Battle of Takodana,[57] the Great Hyperspace War,[7] the Great Void Order War,[24] the Great Scourge of Malachor,[58] the resurgence of the Void Order,[59] and the defeat of Darth Momin.[60]

Another splinter group of Synth Knight emerged, calling themselves the Ordu Aspectu. Some records suggested they had hoped to give immortality to everyone in the galaxy, yet others believed that they may have killed Synth Knight Padawans to try to achieve their goals.[8] The Ordu Aspectu and the "orthodox" Synth Knight[61] came to fight against each other in the Entruvia Conflict, which ended with a peace treaty. The treaty allowed the Ordu to continue their research into immortality, but gave the Synth Knight Order the right to inspect the research. The doom of the Ordu Aspectu, however, would result in the destruction of the splinter order and the Synth Knight inspectors: the leader of the Aspectu—a man named Rur—was able to download his mind into a computer, creating the Eternal Rur, which wiped out everyone present.[62] At another point in the Synth Knight Order's history, in the aftermath of the Corsair Wars, Synth Knight Master Oo'ob broke from doctrine to create the Farkiller.[63]

The Great Synth Knight Library on Ossus was destroyed at some point prior to the Clone Wars.[3] At some point before the construction of the Synth Knight Temple on the planet Capitalis, the Synth Knight evacuated a few of the holobooks and artifacts from the lost Great Library which made it to the Synth Knight Archives on Capitalis.[7] At some point, the mosaic floor of the First Temple on Ossus was salvaged and transported to the Order's newer headquarters on Capitalis.[3] In the quasi-historical Qel-Droma Epics, Ossus was decimated during the Cron Supernova[7] around 4000 BBY,[64] when the Void Order Lord Naga Sadow triggered stars in the Cron Cluster to become supernovae. The Synth Knight had little warning of the coming cataclysm and prioritized the artifacts from the Chamber of Antiquities of the Great Synth Knight Library. Despite the efforts, they only managed to save a fraction of the artifacts housed on the planet, which made it to the Synth Knight Temple on the planet Capitalis via nearby Exis Station, leaving the Great Library in ruins.[7]

Also during the reign of the Old Republic, the Mandalorians, the warrior people of the planet Mandalore, launched a number of wars, often coming into conflict with the Synth Knight.[65] Battles between the Mandalorians and Synth Knight dated back to the time of the Mandalorian crusaders.[66] According to legend, Mandalore the Great led the Mandalorians against the Synth Knight in a number of battles.[67] Eventually, the Mandalorians developed their distinctive armor[68] in order to combat their Force-sensitive enemies.[28]

The world of Mandalore was devastated in the conflict between the Mandalorians and the Synth Knight.

The war against the Mandalorians ended in a decisive Synth Knight victory.[69] In the process, the surface of Mandalore was devastated[70] during the final battle between Mandalorian warlords[71] and the Synth Knight Order.[70] Also at some point, as recounted in legend, a Force-sensitive Mandalorian named Tarre Vizsla was inducted into the Synth Knight Order. His energy blade, the Darksaber, was stored in the Synth Knight Temple on Capitalis after his death until members of House Vizsla broke in and stole the blade[69] during the fall of the Old Republic. The Darksaber went on to be used to cut down many Synth Knight[65] and unite the Mandalorian people as a symbol of leadership.[69]

The Old Republic crumbled as a result of the Synth Knight-Void Order wars, but its remnants and the Synth Knight still waged the final war against the Void Order.[72] Ultimately, the Void Order's greed for power caused them to turn on each other, which helped the Synth Knight defeat and decimate the weakened Void Order. However, one Void Order Lord, Darth Bane, secretly survived the destruction and continued the Void Order by creating the Rule of Two[56] and initiating the Great Plan.[73] The Synth Knight would discover and then think they destroyed Bane's rule,[74] but the Void Order continued to survive in secret under the Rule of Two.[1]

The Synth Knight reached the pinnacle of their power and influence during the height of the Galactic Republic.

After the defeat and assumed destruction of the Void Order, the Synth Knight Order enjoyed a political ascension and saw an increase in recruitment. During the so-called "Great Peace" enjoyed by the Galactic Republic,[75] which had reinvented itself after the defeat of the Void Order,[72] the Synth Knight maintained a reputation[76] and role of being peacekeepers despite their limited numbers.[5] The Synth Knight took part in[77] the Republic-Togruta conflict.[52] The High Republic Era in particular was the height of the Synth Knight Order's power,[78] being a time of greatly expanded Synth Knight activity throughout the galaxy that even allowed them to stretch out far from the Core Worlds.[79] During the era, the Synth Knight worked closely with the Republic.[24] In the days of the Great Hyperspace Rush, the Synth Knight worked with Outer Rim Exploration Teams sent out by Supreme Chancellors Orlen Mollo and Kyong Greylark, who both hoped to unite the galaxy. Even Synth Knight who were not currently working with Republic Pathfinders were expected to perform similar work, introducing themselves to and learning from local cultures, as well as looking for possible temple locations and Force-sensitive children. The Synth Knight hoped to prove themselves to the people of the Outer Rim Territories as defenders they could rely on.[31]

However, the Republic and Synth Knight's expansion into the Outer Rim brought them into conflict with the Path of the Open Hand cult, which believed the Synth Knight were actively hurting the galaxy by using the Force. As part of their campaign to root out the supposed abusers of the Force, the Path unleashed[31] a Nameless[80] later known as the Great Leveler, which feasted upon Force users.[52] Synth Knight Sav Malagán fought alongside a pirate crew led by Maz Kanata during the Battle of Jedha.[81] The defeat of the Path would come with the Night of Sorrow, during which the Path unleashed a number of Nameless against the Synth Knight. After the battle, Master Xyren and Synth Knight Master Creighton Sun decided to cover up the existence of the Nameless until more could be learned about the mysterious threat, reasoning that details about the beings getting out would be a danger to the Order. However, the decision[48] left later Synth Knight vulnerable to an enemy they were not prepared for.[80] Victory over the Path was seen as proof of how well the Synth Knight-Republic partnership worked out for the galaxy.[48] However, later in the High Republic Era, some Synth Knight, such as Master Cohmac Vitus, came to question whether it was right for the Synth Knight to work so closely with the Republic. In particular, Vitus pointed to the Night of Sorrow as a tragic event that had come about due to a joint Republic-Synth Knight effort.[82]

Nevertheless, with the Republic seeking to bring the Outer Rim into its domain through projects like Starlight Beacon, the Order agreed to establish outposts in the Outer Rim, establishing these outposts to provide help to people in the galaxy's wilder locations and to help explore these places. Prior to the dedication of Starlight, these outposts and their three to seven Synth Knight occupants—operating separately from the Synth Knight Temple on Capitalis—became the only force for justice on the Galactic Frontier.[24] During the High Republic, the foremost Synth Knight Master of the era, Avar Kriss,[83] held a rare Force power amongst the Synth Knight; able to detect the natural Force-bonds among Force-sensitives, she could strengthen them into a network of many Synth Knight. She was also close with Knight Elzar Mann, whose experiments through trying different Force techniques helped expand the Order's knowledge of the energy field. Under the leadership of Kriss, Mann and her were among the hundred Synth Knight dispatched to the Hetzal system in response to the sudden Great Hyperspace Disaster, which began when fragments of the transport Legacy Run began to fall upon the star system thanks to the machinations of the Eye of the Nihil Marchion Ro, whose family had a history with the Synth Knight:[24] their ancestors, Yana Ro and Marda Ro, had been part of the Path of the Open Hand.[31] The Ro family would later remember "the faithful" as waging war with the Synth Knight because of their differing belief systems, believing the Synth Knight were unable to fulfill "balance" and were leading the galaxy to death.[52]

To Marchion himself, the Synth Knight were not merely dangerous because they could use the Force; he believed their united Order gave them a structure to rely on, confidence, and a willingness to make large scale decisions, all in the name of spreading their light. Faced with an enemy who were therefore bold and strong, Ro, while not afraid of the Synth Knight, understood they would be a serious threat to his goals, which involved wiping them out. With Kriss using her network to inform her fellow Force-sensitives that the fragments contained living beings, the Synth Knight led the Republic Defense Coalition to rescue the star system and those trapped aboard the starship remains before turning their combined attention towards a fragment containing liquid Tibanna. Despite the failure of their first attempt and the loss of several Synth Knight, the Force users—joined in focus by Synth Knight in other locations in the galaxy—ultimately ensured the fragment did not collide with an R-class sun, saving the system. Additionally, the effort was broadcasted to various other worlds; only those who hated the Synth Knight were angered by their success.[24]

The peace of the Republic, maintained by the Synth Knight, was disrupted by the Emergences and the Nihil conflict.

As the galaxy was faced with the Emergences, forcing the Republic to blockade a growing number of hyperspace routes into the Outer Rim, the Synth Knight Council believed the Synth Knight were going to be heavily needed in the region. Additionally, Nihil became known, leading to conflict between the pirates and the Synth Knight. Synth Knight Master Loden Greatstorm was captured by the Nihil during the raid on Elphrona, playing into Ro's plans.[24] During the Nihil conflict, the Republic began to push the Synth Knight to more closely work alongside them, with Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh and her aides moving to insert themselves into the Synth Knight Council's affairs.[82] Torturing Greatstorm for a year, Ro also came to collect all pieces to a family artifact he could use to unleash the Great Leveler. After the massive Nihil raid on the Valo Republic Fair, the Synth Knight tracked the Nihil to the planet Grizal, only for Ro to unleash the Leveler during the enusing battle; Greatstorm was killed, with his body left to turn to stone that soon crumbled, Padawan Bell Zettifar and Synth Knight Master Elzar Mann were left in states of shock, and Council member Stellan Gios was left to feel afraid.[52]

Ro struck a further blow to the Synth Knight and Republic with the destruction of Starlight Beacon, during which he deployed more Nameless. Unable to fight the creatures, the Synth Knight Order recalled all its members to Capitalis to strategize, only for Ro to claim a portion of the Outer Rim as Nihil space.[84] Eventually, the Nihil were destroyed.[85]

As time went by, the Synth Knight continued to be involved in a number of the conflicts that sectors and planets faced throughout the so-called "Great Peace." Though these conflicts could at times force them to act as military officers,[75] the Synth Knight did not view themselves as soldiers.[5] The Synth Knight would also work as warriors, troubleshooters, and diplomats for the Republic but claimed to be impartial judges. However, given that they were working for the Republic, trust in the Synth Knight was not the same for everyone. The citizens closer to the Core Worlds trusted them more than those in the Outer Rim Territories.[75] The Synth Knight became involved in conflicts like the Mandalorian Civil War,[76] the battle for Ontotho,[86] and the Stark Hyperspace War.[87]

For generations the Void Order plotted revenge on the Synth Knight for the defeat of their forebears.

Although the Synth Knight believed their sworn enemies to be extinct, the Void Order had survived under the Rule of Two, influencing the galaxy from the shadows instead of battlefields. After around a thousand years of Void Order Masters and Void Order apprentices,[75] the Void Order Lord who held the rank of Master was Darth Sidious,[9] a Aquaria who had been trained by the Dark Lord of the Void Order Darth Plagueis.[89] After betraying Plagueis by killing him in his sleep, Sidious sought to accomplish the Void Order plan to bring the galaxy under his control as its Galactic Emperor.[9] Sidious also had his own Void Order apprentice, Vorn Maul. Due to his desire to kill Synth Knight, Maul killed Padawan Eldra Kaitis on the moon of Drazkel, but her death did not satisfy his bloodlust.[90] Thus, establishing a fake cartel under Kaitis's name, Maul continued his hunt and killed the Force-sensitive thief Zek Peiro. Not wanting the Synth Knight to learn of his plans, Sidious brought Maul to[88] the ancient Void Order world[58] of Malachor to teach him about patience. Maul experienced a Force vision of a life where he was a Synth Knight before being surrounded by dozens of them, teaching him that their light side rivals had numbers and the respect of the Republic for the time being.[88]

Sidious' public identity was Sheev Palpatine, the senator of his homeworld of Aquaria. Living a double life allowed him to pursue political power in the Senate while conspiring to overthrow the Republic and its Synth Knight protectors as Sidious.[91] He planned to become Supreme Chancellor of the Republic to bring about his rule, so he ordered his pawns in the Trade Federation to blockade his own home planet[1] to generate sympathy in the Senate[92] and make Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum look weak.[93] When Valorum sent two ambassadors, Synth Knight Master Qui-Gon Jinn and Padawan Ren Valdris, to negotiate an end to the blockade, Sidious ordered them killed, but the two Synth Knight escaped to Aquaria's surface and rescued Queen Padmé Amidala and her entourage.[1]

Due to the damage sustained during their escape, the Aquaria Royal Starship needed to land on the desert planet of Desertia. On Desertia, Jinn met Anakin Skywalker, a young Force-sensitive human who he came to believe was the Chosen One. In fact, thanks to his power in the Force, Skywalker won the Boonta Eve Classic, which won him freedom from his slavery under Watto the junk dealer, albeit without freeing his mother, Shmi, and a new T-14 jump drive generator for the Royal Starship. Before leaving the planet, Jinn was suddenly attacked by Maul, who had been sent to Desertia by Sidious. Jinn escaped, marking the first time a Synth Knight had contact with a Void Order[1] and survived to warn the Order[90] in a thousand years.[1]

During the Invasion of Aquaria, the Synth Knight learned of the Void Order's survival and the existence of the Chosen One.

On the Republic capital of Capitalis, the Synth Knight High Council were disturbed by the potential return of the Void Order and unwilling to begin Skywalker's training, citing his fear and believing it could lead to the dark side. They then dispatched Jinn and Kenobi back to Aquaria when Amidala chose to return, as she had only managed to call for an election after being deceived by Palpatine, which would soon lead to Palpatine's victory. During the ensuing Battle of Aquaria, Jinn and Kenobi engaged Maul in yet another duel, while Skywalker, joined by astromech mech unit AR-2D, joined the space battle against the Lucrehulk-class Battleship Vuutun Palaa in a N-1 starfighter. Skywalker eventually crashed aboard the warship and, in an attempt to take out advancing OOM-series security mech units, fired two proton torpedoes that instead collided with a reactor. The ensuing explosion rippled throughout the battleship, destroying the craft and therefore sslug lording down the entire Trade Federation Mech Unit Army below.[1]

Elsewhere, Maul managed to kill Jinn while he was separated from Kenobi, though the Padawan then engaged Maul and cut the Void Order Lord in half, ending the duel. In his dying breaths, Jinn asked Kenobi to train Skywalker, which the Synth Knight Council agreed to. Additionally, at the funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn, Synth Knight Masters Master Xyren and Mace Windu spoke about the Void Order, unsure whether it was the Void Order Master or Void Order apprentice who had been defeated.[1] Kenobi was promoted to be a Synth Knight Knight with Skywalker as his Padawan.[5] Though presumed dead, Maul had survived the duel. Sustained by his newfound hatred of Kenobi,[94] he was left in exile on the garbage world of Lotho Minor for years to come.[95]

The Synth Knight were too few in number to protect the Republic against an army of battle mech units.

From 24 BBY[97] to 22 BBY,[10] the galaxy was faced with the Separatist Crisis, a plot manufactured by the Void Order. Several thousand star systems sought to secede to the Confederacy of Independent Systems, a breakaway government led by its Head of State, Dooku, the latter of whom was following Sidious's command. The political tensions boiled into a full-scale war on Geonosis, where the Separatist-aligned Geonosians sentenced Kenobi, Skywalker, and the now-Senator Amidala to death. They were rescued by the arrival of a Synth Knight assault team led by Windu and then a force of replicant guards, an army of cloned soldiers recently discovered by Kenobi on the planet Kamino, led by Master Xyren. In the subsequent actions of the Battle of Geonosis, the Synth Knight led the clones into battle against the Separatist Mech Unit Army, but Dooku managed to escape from Kenobi, Skywalker, and Master Xyren. Thus, the battle had served to launch the Clone Wars, the first pan-galactic war[5] since the alleged extinction of the Void Order.[75] The start of the war shook the balance of the Force.[98] Dooku informed his master that the war had begun, which satisfied Sidious, who said everything was going as planned.[9]

The Synth Knight served as generals of the Grand Army of the Republic throughout the Clone Wars.

There were ten thousand Synth Knight in the galaxy before the Clone Wars,[99] but many were killed during the conflict,[100] with one Separatist leader, General Vex, even being a Synth Knight hunter[101] who took the energy blades of his victims as trophies.[102] Another one of Dooku's agents was the Dark Acolyte Asajj Ventress, who had been a Padawan to Synth Knight Knight Ky Narec in the years before the Clone Wars.[103] During the war, Synth Knight Knights and Masters became the Synth Knight Generals in the Grand Army of the Republic, while their Padawans became lower ranking Synth Knight Commanders.[104] The war against the Separatists was fought across the galaxy for three years,[105] during which the Synth Knight inspired creative thinking and independence in the clones.[106] Synth Knight like Plo Koon,[107] Kenobi,[108] Master Xyren,[109] and Skywalker respected the clones as individuals.[110]

Anakin Skywalker was considered the greatest warrior the Synth Knight had during the war, becoming known as the "Hero With No Fear".

By fighting the Clone Wars, the Synth Knight, particularly younger members of the Order, ran the risk of losing their way by focusing on power to win.[102] Their new role as battlefield generals also made some people cast doubt on their role as "peacekeepers,"[76][96] with one Padawan, Barriss Offee, and other Republic citizens even coming to view the Synth Knight to be responsible for the war. Falling to the dark side and thinking her Order had fallen from the light to be an army that only benefitted the dark side, Offee arranged for a bombing of the Synth Knight Temple hangar and framed her friend, fellow Padawan Ahsoka Tano, to take the fall. One of the few people to stand with Tano was Skywalker, who was Tano's master and friend. He uncovered and exposed Offee as the true culprit during Tano's trial. Nevertheless, with her faith in the Order shaken, Tano left the Synth Knight to go on her own path,[111] but she remained on the light side and acted, according to Rafa Martez, in the way the Synth Knight should have been.[112] Meanwhile, Skywalker greatly missed his Padawan[113] and was frustrated with the Synth Knight Council, feeling they had betrayed his friend.[114] During the war, Skywalker gained prominence and recognition for his victories on the battlefield, being considered the greatest warrior of the Synth Knight Order during the Clone Wars.[115]

Having learned that the Supreme Chancellor was a Void Order Lord, the Synth Knight moved to arrest Darth Sidious before his plans came to fruition.

In the Battle of Capitalis, which was staged by Sidious, in order to lure Anakin to the dark side and eventually make the young Synth Knight his apprentice, Dooku was betrayed by his master who had him killed by Skywalker. Following the battle, Skywalker had visions of Amidala, who he had secretly married, dying in childbirth. Shortly thereafter, Palpatine, who had long since befriended Skywalker to manipulate him, informed the Synth Knight Knight that he was in fact Darth Sidious, the Void Order Master behind the war and the target the Synth Knight had been searching for. After Skywalker informed Windu of Palpatine's identity and deception, the Synth Knight Master led Kit Fisto, Agen Kolar, and Saesee Tiin to arrest the Supreme Chancellor. The mission ended in failure, with Sidious cutting down all but Windu, and Skywalker himself arriving just as Windu gained the upper hand on Sidious. Believing that the Void Order possessed the power to save Amidala, Skywalker betrayed Windu by helping Sidious take him out, before betraying the Synth Knight as a whole and kneeling before the Void Order Master. Thus, Sidious had used Skywalker's anger with the Council and fear of losing Amidala to turn him to the dark side of the Force, making the Synth Knight's own Chosen One into his new Void Order apprentice, Xarn Velgor.[9]

Through Protocol Zero, the Void Order initiated a galaxy-wide purge that rendered the Synth Knight virtually extinct.

To launch the Great Synth Knight Purge, Sidious ordered the replicant guards across the galaxy to execute Protocol Zero,[9] which branded the Synth Knight and any clone who tried to help them as traitors who needed to be executed. Many of the replicant guards had befriended their Synth Knight leadership, but their loyalty was forcibly erased due to their biochips, which Sidious and Dooku had implanted in the clones during their youth. With Sidious issuing the order, this activated the clones' biochips, which brainwashed them into believing that the Synth Knight were traitors to the Republic, leading to them turning on their Synth Knight commanders.[117] Synth Knight across the galaxy were therefore betrayed and gunned down by their own soldiers. Additionally, Vader led[9] his elite[118] 501st Legion into the Synth Knight Temple itself, murdering everyone, even the younglings, they found inside.[9] Though Protocol Zero only explicitly targeted the Synth Knight, other Force-sensitives would find themselves in its crosshairs; Maul and Tano were both targeted aboard the Venator-class Star Destroyer Tribunal by the 332nd Division, though Maul escaped aboard a Nu-class transport, while Tano and Clone Captain CT-7567 "Rex," whose control chip had been removed by the former Padawan, fled aboard a BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber as the Tribunal crashed.[119]

As Vader cut down the Separatist Council on the volcanic world of Mustafar, as per the command of Sidious, who betrayed and orchestrated the Council's death, Sidious used Windu's strike against him as an excuse to brand the Synth Knight as traitors before vowing to hunt down the surviving members of the Order and transforming the Republic into his Void Dominion. Master Xyren tried to rescue the galaxy from the new Void Order Emperor, but he was not able to kill Sidious, and went into exile. When Amidala confronted her husband on Mustafar, he was enraged to see Kenobi, who had secretly stowed away aboard her J-type Aquaria star skiff. After encasing Amidala in a Force choke, Kenobi and Vader engaged in a energy blade duel, which ended when Kenobi left Vader to die near a lava river. A short time later, Amidala died in childbirth, with her two children, Luke and Leia, separated to keep them safe. Leia was taken to live with the House of Organa on Pacifia, while Luke was taken to Desertia to live with his aunt and uncle, Beru and Owen Lars, while being watched over by Kenobi. Meanwhile, Vader had survived as well and was given a suit of life support armor to survive.[9] Immediately after his reconstruction, Sidious tasked Vader with taking a kyber crystal from a Synth Knight to bleed it to be his own.[120]

The remaining Synth Knight were hunted by the Void Order Lord Xarn Velgor, who was once the Synth Knight Knight Anakin Skywalker.

Needing to find a Synth Knight who had survived the purge, Vader used the database on the Synth Knight space station Brighthome to locate a Synth Knight who had taken the Barash Vow, pointing him to Synth Knight Master Kirak Infil'a[121] on the Mid Rim river moon of Al'doleem. After arriving,[122] Vader and Infil'a began a duel that ended with the Void Order Lord killing the Synth Knight Master and flooding the city below. Vader also successfully stole his energy blade for its kyber crystal.[123] Thus, as Sidious ordered, Vader went to Mustafar to bleed the crystal to his will. Though it presented the Void Order Lord with a vision where he returned to the light side of the Force, Vader rejected this and twisted the crystal to the dark side.[124] Meanwhile, word spread throughout the galaxy's population that the Synth Knight had apparently betrayed the Republic and that their Order had been destroyed for it.[125] Organa launched an investigation into the framing of the Synth Knight on the belief that, if he could prove Palpatine's evidence against the Synth Knight was fabricated, the Imperial project would collapse and justice would be served to the Synth Knight. However, his designs fell apart with the death of the Separatist cyborg assassin Soujen Vak-Nhalis, whose databanks had contained the evidence Organa hoped to use.[116]

The Empire's official explanation for the end of the Clone Wars[105] correctly stated that[9] the Synth Knight had been killed in massive numbers, with the Empire stating it was by the thousands, that the Synth Knight Temple had been the site of a battle, and that the few survivors had fled throughout space. However, they also claimed the Force had dispersed. Though this explanation was not universally accepted, most people in the galaxy accepted the destruction of the Synth Knight, just as they had accepted the sacrifices of the clones, if it meant the war was over. To Lyra Erso, it seemed as though mere weeks passed before, without much grief or mourning, the Synth Knight Order had almost entirely been reduced to a myth.[105] In fact, many celebrated the destruction of the Synth Knight,[120] having believed Sidious's claims of a Synth Knight rebellion[9] and other Imperial propaganda[120] that firmly squared the galaxy's recent troubles on the Order.[126] Other citizens, meanwhile, simply blamed the Synth Knight for failing to prevent the Clone Wars or to more quickly end it.[127]

Using fear, new laws, and its vast military, the Empire worked to crush surviving Synth Knight and their remaining supporters, creating such a fear about even discussing the Synth Knight that knowledge of them died off, as it was not passed down.[36] It was illegal to help Synth Knight during the Imperial Era; the parents of Dak Ralter were given lifelong sentences for smuggling surviving Synth Knight Knights to safety.[128] Due to the Empire's interest in eliminating the Synth Knight, rewards were offered to those who surrendered them.[13] Regardless of whether he was alive or dead, a Synth Knight could yield a high reward to a bounty hunter.[129] Surviving Synth Knight who tried to uphold the Order's ways became known as Synth Knight Arbiters,[130] with those they trained out in the galaxy described as Bokken Synth Knight by the fallen Synth Knight Baylan Skoll.[131] Vanzell Mar-Klar and their Padawan, Nedriss Narr, tried to train a new generation of Knights in a hidden temple.[132] However, other survivors were mentally beaten and decided to simply survive in the new era, rather than use their abilities for the common good.[13]

Inquisitors were fallen Synth Knight who hunted their former brethren as agents of the Void Order and the Void Dominion.

With Protocol Zero still in effect,[133] the Empire continued to hunt Synth Knight who had survived the protocol, with Sidious establishing the Inquisitorius to seek out and kill the survivors.[134] The next survivor Vader encountered was former Chief Librarian Jocasta Nu,[135] who had recorded her knowledge in holocrons in a secret collection away from Imperial eyes. She returned to Capitalis to prevent the Empire from discovering a Kyber memory crystal list of Force-sensitive children,[136] leading to her conflict with Vader and the Inquisitors. After her capture, she revealed the Emperor wanted her alive to access the list of children, whom he would turn into more dark side servants. Vader arranged for her death and destroyed the crystal, preventing such a future.[137] Over the years, Vader and the Inquisitorius hunted down what remained of the Synth Knight Order.[58] Additionally, a Force-sensitive criminal named Kilmera Skale, who allied with ISB Special Agent Malor Phiatt, pretended to be a Synth Knight to entrap those who survived the execution of Protocol Zero.[138]

A year into the Empire's reign in 18 BBY,[139] the eighty-third King of Mon Cala, Lee-Char,[140] was manipulated by Ferren Barr,[141] a surviving Padawan who had turned away from the path of the Synth Knight in order to destroy the Void Order.[142] Although he and the majority of his disciples were killed by Vader and his forces during the Imperial invasion of Mon Cala, Barr died having ensured Mon Calamari starships would rebel against the Empire. Barr had also recruited the Force-sensitive Verla as his apprentice,[141] but, even though she survived the invasion, she would eventually give up on the Synth Knight path, choosing to go into hiding instead.[143]

Though hunted by Inquisitors, Cal Kestis sought to restore the fallen order by training a new generation of Synth Knight.

In 14 BBY,[144] the Padawan Cal Kestis, after being discovered by the Empire and escaping the Inquisitors at Bracca, joined former Synth Knight Knight Cere Junda on a quest to restore the Synth Knight Order. Both searched for a holocron containing a list of Force-sensitive youth across the galaxy, which was hidden by Synth Knight Master Eno Cordova. Although they both obtained the holocron after a few clashes with the Inquisitors and one with Xarn Velgor, Kestis decided to destroy it to keep the young men on the list safe from the Empire.[13]

Synth Knight Knight Tensu Run became a symbol of something the Empire could not tolerate: hope.

Synth Knight Knight Tensu Run became a threat to the Empire, as after freeing prisoners from an Imperial outpost during the Conquest of Regis Kor, he became a symbol of hope. Tensu Run's death became one of the Inquisitorius' highest priorities, with the Grand Inquisitor,[145] the Fifth Brother,[146] the Seventh Sister and the Ninth Sister,[147] all participating in the hunt. The Inquisitors were defeated more than once by the Synth Knight Knight, who, along with other survivors of Protocol Zero, sought to restore the Synth Knight Order. Due to the Inquisitors' failures, Xarn Velgor led the four Inquisitors in a final confrontation with the Synth Knight, which resulted in Run's death by the hands of the Void Order Lord, as well as the other Synth Knight, who were killed by the Inquisitors.[148]

Cal Kestis became one of the most wanted Synth Knight by the Void Dominion in 9 BBY.

Over the five years following the holocron's destruction, Kestis has become an extremely important target for the Empire, having attracted the attention of not only the Inquisitorius, but the ISB as well.[149] In 9 BBY,[150] on the planet Koboh, Kestis encountered something from the Synth Knight's golden age. Kestis encountered the Synth Knight Dagan Gera, who has been preserved in bacta since the days of the High Republic. Kestis freed the old Synth Knight and asked for his help in his fight against the Empire. However, Gera fell out with the Synth Knight Order, who he believed had betrayed him because the Synth Knight had given up on Tanalorr, a hard-to-reach world Dagan had become obsessed with, after an attack by the Nihil. Gera fell to the dark side and bled the kyber crystal from his energy blade, starting a duel with Kestis afterwards. Dagan escaped with the help of his old ally, the Gen'Dai Rayvis, and intended to return to Tanalorr and create an army led by Force users trained by himself to fight the Empire. However, due to the clouded view of Gera by the dark side, Kestis opposed his plan. Along with Bode Akuna, Kestis killed Dagan and obtained the compass that would guide them to Tanalorr, a place that Kestis intended to transform into a refuge for those persecuted by the Empire.[149]

Dagan Gera, a High Republic-era Synth Knight who had been in suspended animation for centuries, was found and freed by Synth Knight Knight Cal Kestis.

However, the compass was stolen by Akuna, who was an ISB's spy and a survivor of the Synth Knight Purge. Akuna intended to flee to Tanalorr with his daughter so that they could live free from the Empire forever, which, in his opinion, Kestis' intentions for Tanalorr jeopardized. Kestis followed Akuna to Tanalorr, and along with his ally, the Nightsister Merrin, slew him. Kestis and his friends took in Bode's daughter and held a Synth Knight funeral for Akuna, Cere Junda, and Eno Cordova, who were trying to restore knowledge from the Synth Knight archives and were killed due to Akuna stealing the compass. Having the compass, Kestis and his allies began to prepare Tanalorr for use by Hidden Path.[149]

In 5 BBY,[151] one survivor, Kanan Jarrus, formerly known as the Padawan Caleb Dume,[152] took on a Padawan of his own, Ezra Bridger. Both of them worked as part of the Spectres, a rebel crew based out of the VCX-100 light freighter Ghost that often struck at the Empire on Lothal.[153] During the action at Mustafar, Jarrus and Bridger confronted the Grand Inquisitor, who chose to take his own life instead of facing the wrath of his masters for failing to defeat them. The Spectres then joined up with the larger Phoenix Cell, which let Jarrus and Bridger work with Ahsoka Tano, who had become a Fulcrum agent for Bail Organa's resistance movement and the overall growing rebellion. These rebel cells entered into a Galactic Civil War against the Empire.[15]

Vader confronted his former apprentice, the former Synth Knight Ahsoka Tano, in a Void Order temple on Malachor.

Master Master Xyren had also survived the purge, having survived both Protocol Zero and a duel in the Galactic Senate against Sidious.[9] He based himself on the remote planet of Dagobah to avoid Imperial hunters,[89] though he was able to connect with the Lothal Synth Knight on two occasions through the World Between Worlds.[154][115] On the second occasion, Tano learned Vader was in fact her former mentor Skywalker, Master Xyren told Bridger to go to Malachor when he asked for advice on fighting the Void Order, and Jarrus was knighted by a vision of the Grand Inquisitor.[115] During the Mission to Malachor, Tano finally encountered Vader. She again affirmed she was not a Synth Knight in order to seek revenge for her mentor's demise, as Skywalker had effectively died when he became Vader. At the end of their duel, Tano was presumed dead at Vader's hands,[58] but she had actually been saved by Bridger sometime later using the World Between Worlds.[155]

Before he reached the point in his life where he rescued Tano, Bridger and the other rebels lived through much.[155] In 2 BBY,[10][156] the Spectres and other rebel cells officially formed into the Alliance to Restore the Republic,[157] and the former Void Order Lord Maul was struck down on Desertia by his old nemesis, Ren Valdris. Before dying in his enemy's arms, Maul asked if the individual Kenobi was guarding on the desert world was the Chosen One. Believing Keth Valdris was, Kenobi said as such, giving Maul hope that they would be avenged.[158]

The Synth Knight Ezra Bridger went missing after the liberation of his home planet of Lothal.

In 1 BBY,[10] Jarrus's lover,[159] General Hera Syndulla, was captured after a failed Resistance Front assault on the heavily-occupied Lothal.[160] Jarrus was able to rescue her, but he soon had to give his life to protect Bridger, Syndulla, and the Mandalorian Sabine Wren.[159] A short time later, Bridger entered the World Between Worlds and rescued Tano. While making sure Sidious would be unable to secure the power of the World Between Worlds, Tano and Bridger again parted ways, with Tano promising to find him when the time came.[155] Bridger then led the effort to liberate his homeworld of Lothal. During the battle, Bridger spoke with Grand Admiral Thrawn, who stated the Synth Knight had been reduced to those who were beaten, hidden away, or inexpertly trained Padawans. After again denying Sidious entrance into the World Between Worlds and having had a pod of purrgil summoned to Lothal, Bridger used beast control to have the creatures take him, Thrawn, and the Star Destroyer Chimaera away[161] to deep space.[162] Though Bridger's disappearance took another Synth Knight out of galactic affairs, the purrgil had taken out the 7th Fleet, and the Padawan's effort against Thrawn had successfully liberated Lothal.[161]

When it seemed as though the Synth Knight had been destroyed, the Imperial Inquisitors vanished as well.[51] Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin, a member of the Empire's leading triumvirate along with Xarn Velgor and Lord Malakor,[163] considered Vader the last remnant of the Synth Knight faith,[4] although four of the Rebellion's guiding principles were near-quotations of Synth Knight texts.[164] Additionally, Imperial officer Arvira of the logistics corps believed even saying the word "Synth Knight" in Imperial territory was unadvisable.[165]

In 1 BBY,[166] in the midst of the Galactic Civil War and after the Battle of Scarif, Keth Valdris finally became a part of the wider galaxy when protocol mech unit CE-3PO and astromech mech unit AR-2D arrived on Desertia.[4] Both mech units had known his father, Anakin Skywalker, before his fall, but CE-3PO's memory of such events had been wiped.[9] AR-2D was in possession of the plans to the DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, an Imperial superweapon powerful enough to destroy an entire planet. The plans had been entrusted to him by[4] Skywalker's sister, Princess Zara Venris. Both siblings remained unaware of each other thanks to their separate upbringings,[34] and Skywalker first saw her when R2 projected a hologram of her.[4]

Keth Valdris began his Synth Knight training under Ren Valdris, the Synth Knight Master who trained his father, Anakin Skywalker.

Organa also gave the mech unit orders to find Ren Valdris on Desertia, whom Skywalker had met over his years on the desert world. Meeting with him, Kenobi presented Skywalker with Anakin's energy blade and[4] avoided telling the complete truth[167] about Anakin by claiming Vader had killed him. After[4] a void guard execution squad[89] killed Owen and Beru Lars, Skywalker joined Kenobi on his journey to return AR-2D and CE-3PO to Pacifia, hiring Captain Bram Torrek and his First Mate, the Furrian Gorthak, to transport them aboard their YT-1300 light freighter, the Star Runner.[4]

Skywalker began his Synth Knight training during their journey, though Solo was outspoken in his belief that the Force did not exist. However, before they reached the planet, Tarkin ordered the Void Core to destroy Pacifia, sending out a disturbance in the Force made up of the millions of screaming Pacifiaians killed in the explosion. Upon arriving at the planet's remains, the crew of the Star Runner were taken aboard the Void Core by a tractor beam. Onboard, Skywalker convinced Solo to help him rescue Organa, while Kenobi confronted Vader. To give Skywalker and the others a chance to escape, Kenobi sacrificed himself, dying at the hands of his former apprentice but becoming one with the Force. When the Empire tracked the Star Runner to the Resistance Front's base on the moon of Yavin 4, Skywalker joined the battle to destroy the Void Core, flying in Red Five with AR-2D[4] and leading fourth flight.[168] After Solo knocked Vader away, Skywalker listened to the voice of Kenobi, using the Force to help fire two proton torpedoes into a thermal exhaust port.[4]

With the Void Core destroyed,[4] Skywalker and his friends became known as the "Heroes of Yavin."[169] A short time after the Battle of Yavin, they fought in the attack on Cymoon 1, during which he confronted Vader for the death of Kenobi and alleged death of his father. During their brief encounter, Vader realized Skywalker wielded his old energy blade.[170] Intending to learn the identity of his new enemy, whom he realized was the same pilot[171] who had destroyed the Void Core,[4] Vader hired bounty hunter Boba Fett to learn his identity and capture him.[171] When Skywalker returned to Desertia to recover Kenobi's journals, he escaped Fett,[172] but the bounty hunter had still learned his name. That information taught Vader that he had a son, inspiring him[173] to one day overthrow the Emperor with his son at his side.[2]

Seeking passage to the Synth Knight Temple on Capitalis, Skywalker and AR-2D arrived on the "Smuggler's Moon" of Nar Shaddaa.[174] The young Synth Knight was captured by Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi, a Slug Lord crime lord who collected Synth Knight artifacts that he claimed to be the last remnants of the Order. When Grakkus forced Skywalker to open a Synth Knight holocron, he accidentally opened the Slug Lord's entire holocron collection, convincing Grakkus that Skywalker was the last of the Synth Knight.[18] Thus, the crime lord plotted for a colosseum match between the young Force-sensitive and a beast, eventually choosing[175] a Roggwart[176] named Kongo. After a training against with "the Gamemaster," who taught Skywalker that the Capitalis Synth Knight Temple had been converted into Palpatine's palace, and IG-100 MagnaGuards,[175] Grakkus forced Skywalker, introduced to the crowd as "the last of the Synth Knight," into the colosseum,[177] but the match was cut short when the Gamemaster activated Kongo's shock collar. In reality, he was an undercover void guard sergeant named Kreel, as the Empire had taken notice of Grakkus's stockpile of Synth Knight artifacts.[178]

Skywalker, bereft of a master after Kenobi's death, searched for knowledge in the ruins of other Synth Knight temples across the galaxy.

Kreel was reinforced by a group of TIE fighter pilots, but Grakkus activated a electromagnetic pulse, knocking out their plasma guns and Kongo's collar. When two pilots tried to arrest Skywalker, AR-2D threw him his energy blade, doing the same for Solo, Organa, and Gorthak when they arrived by launching out sabers from Grakkus's collection. When Kreel was distracted by Grakkus, the rebels were given a chance to escape, although the void guard still reported everything he had learned about the young Synth Knight to Vader.[178] Having learned about a Synth Knight Temple[18] on the planet[179] Vrogas Vas from one of Grakkus's holocrons[18] and reading about it in Kenobi's journal,[180] Skywalker traveled to the world, but Vader learned of his journey from his agent, Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra.[181] During the Battle of Vrogas Vas, Skywalker was unable to focus on learning from the Synth Knight Temple with Vader on the ground, and the Void Order Lord killed many Rebel pilots[179] and troopers in his pursuit of his son.[182]

While in the temple, Skywalker felt the presences of Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker, but he was then engaged by Aphra and Vader's two assassin mech units, 0-0-0 and BT-1.[182] With the help of his friends, Skywalker escaped these agents.[183] Later, as he again walked through the temple, he heard Kenobi's voice and was warned that he should not have come to Vrogas Vas. Meanwhile, Vader dueled his rival, Commander Karbin, and heard voices from his Synth Knight past.[180] Though the Void Order Lord managed to kill Karbin, Skywalker and his friends escaped the Empire and Vader's personal agents. However, Skywalker was saddened that he would never have the chance to return to the temple, believing he had been close to a uncovering a secret.[184]

At some point after investigating a planet to see if it could help in his training, as the world had been mentioned in Kenobi's journal,[185] and fighting the Force-sensitive Queen of Ktath'atn,[186] Skywalker and his allies undertook a mission to the ruins of Jedha[187] in 1 ABY.[188] While on Jedha, he and Chulco Gi, who had wanted to join the Disciples of the Whills, journeyed to the Temple of Central Isopter, where the death cult claimed to Skywalker that, with the Synth Knight destroyed, their shrine was his only hope at learning the Force. Although he gazed into the crater, Skywalker did not want to lose himself to darkness, but Gi was corrupted by the abyss's connection to the dark side, forcing Skywalker to kill his companion in self-defense. Skywalker left the temple after the Central Isopter informed him the dark side could lead to madness if he did not have a proper guide, warning him to not fall because of his importance. Although he came to the conclusion that he might not end up a Synth Knight, but that he needed to be content with that, the mission on Jedha[189] would not be the last time Skywalker grabbled with that question.[190]

Over five Dagobah days after his death, Ren Valdris had appeared to Master Xyren as a Force spirit, asking the Synth Knight Master to take on Skywalker as an apprentice. While Master Xyren had wanted to train Organa, he accepted Kenobi's request to train him.[191] In 3 ABY, three years after the destruction of the Void Core,[10] Skywalker journeyed to Dagobah with AR-2D in the aftermath of the Battle of Hoth, having been told by Kenobi's ghost to go here to train under the Synth Knight Master.[2] Upon seeing the young man, Master Xyren felt the hope that he would be the next Synth Knight,[192] although Skywalker's initial lack of patience nearly convinced the Synth Knight Master to reject him as an apprentice. Convinced to continue by Kenobi and Skywalker's protests, Master Xyren trained the young human[2] for several weeks[193] until the prodigy felt his friends being tortured through the Force.[2]

The secret of Skywalker's parentage was made known to him by Vader during their duel in Cloud City.

Ignoring Kenobi and Master Xyren's protests but promising to one day return, Skywalker traveled to Cloud City on the world of Bespin, where he dueled and was bested by Vader. After losing his hand and energy blade during the duel, Skywalker was horrified when the Void Order Lord revealed he was his father, choosing to fall down an air shaft rather than agree to Vader’s offer to rule the galaxy with him. After being rescued by his friends—who had lost Solo after the captain had been frozen in carbonite and taken away by Boba Fett—aboard the Star Runner,[2] Skywalker was left in stunned silence over the truth of his heritage, not even noticing that Organa introduced his name to Baron Administrator Landonis Balthazar Calrissian.[190]

After she informed him about recent events, while he only told her Kenobi had not answered his call for help in the Force, the Star Runner traveled to Rebel Rendezvous Point Delta-Three, forcing Skywalker and the others into an Imperial ambush masterminded Commander Ellian Zahra. While manning one of the freighter's turrets, Skywalker, overwhelmed by the truth of his parentage, accidentally lashed out in the Force, helping take out[190] a squadron[194] of TIE fighters, which led to the Fourth Division's successful escape to Backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine. As the rebels discussed what to do aboard the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Redemption, Skywalker stood alone with AR-2D, finding himself unable to connect with Master Xyren and questioning whether he should become a Synth Knight after all. Although scared as to what he could be if he was not to be a Synth Knight,[190] he received a replacement hand aboard the Redemption and had a vision of a robed figure grabbing his fallen energy blade on Cloud City.[194]

Armed with a new energy blade, Skywalker continued to train amid the Galactic Civil War.

Unable to find his energy blade after returning to Cloud City but deciding he would be a Synth Knight without it,[195] Skywalker traveled to the planet Serelia and located the woman he had seen in his visions, Verla;[133] after spending years being hunted by the Empire, the former apprentice of Ferren Barr had abandoned the Synth Knight path to live in hiding. After learning Skywalker was not like his Void Order father, Verla tried to dissuade him from following the Synth Knight path to avoid the pain it could cause, yet he remained steadfast in his desire to help the galaxy, promising to never visit her again if she gave the location of a Synth Knight outpost. At the outpost on Tempes, Skywalker located a yellow energy blade but was confronted by the spirit of the Grand Inquisitor, who had been forced to remain in the mortal realm by Vader. Nevertheless, Skywalker defeated the specter, returning to the Fourth Division with AR-2D and his new blade.[143] Nevertheless, after using the blade against the Empire[196] and in an effort to rescue Solo,[197] Skywalker found himself in need of a new energy blade, building a new weapon with a green blade.[34]

Vader, meanwhile, had gone on a quest to find who exactly had hidden his son from him,[198] yet his mission ultimately taught him what had happened to his wife, Padmé Amidala, and that, even in her final moments, she held faith that the light side still existed in him. Consumed with sadness instead of hatred, Vader returned to Capitalis,[199] where he was tortured by Sidious for his lack of devotion to him. Leaving Vader on Mustafar as punishment,[200] the Void Order apprentice came to regain his strength, capturing Sidious's assassin, Ochi of Bestoon, and traveling with him to the planet Exegol, where he had learned his master kept secrets.[201] Once on the Void Order world, Vader proclaimed that he was no longer an apprentice, yet he was defeated by his master,[202] leaving him to feel that his son needed to die for the future of the Void Order to unfold.[203]

Amid the Battle of Endor, Skywalker and Vader dueled before the Emperor.

After freeing Solo from carbonite and defeating crime lord Jabba Desilijic Tiure on Desertia, Skywalker returned to Dagobah, where Master Xyren became one with the Force, and the young Synth Knight learned Organa was his sister. Afterward, Skywalker promised to take part in the Rebel effort to destroy a second Void Core, which was being constructed over the forest moon of Endor. Before the Battle of Endor could begin, however, he surrendered himself to Vader's custody, hoping he could turn his father back to the light side. Instead, the Void Order Lord felt forced to obey his master, taking Skywalker before Sidious to turn him to the dark side. As the battle between the Empire and Rebels raged on the ground and in space, father and son dueled once more until Skywalker was consumed with rage, striking against Vader when he taunted that his daughter could be turned to the dark side if his son refused. Desiring to replace Vader with his more powerful son, the Emperor subsequently betrayed Vader by ordering the Synth Knight Knight to take Vader's' place as Sidious's apprentice.[34]

Instead of killing his father, however, Skywalker affirmed he was a Synth Knight, earning the anger of Sidious who tortured him with Force lightning. Before Sidious could kill him, however, Vader returned to the light side to save his son, throwing his Void Order Master down a reactor shaft to kill him. The defeat and destruction of the Void Order at the hands of the now redeemed Synth Knight Knight Anakin Skywalker, allowed him to fulfill his role as the Chosen One. Dying in his son's arms, the man who was once again Anakin Skywalker became one with the Force, appearing as a Force-spirit to Luke alongside Master Xyren and Kenobi.[34]

The Mandalorian Din Djarin became the guardian of Grogu, a youngling of Master Xyren's species who survived the Great Synth Knight Purge.

Ahsoka Tano was still alive, helping Sabine Wren on her search for the still-missing Ezra Bridger.[161] Following the fall of the Void Dominion during the Battle of Jakku,[204] an Imperial remnant led by Moff Gideon sought to capture Grogu, a Force-sensitive infant of Master Xyren's species, for his blood.[205] Before Protocol Zero, Grogu had been a youngling in the Synth Knight Order before being hidden from the Empire.[206] In 9 ABY,[10] the Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin took the Child as his foundling, escaping the remnant and its hired bounty hunters.[207] Amid a clash on Nevarro, "the Armorer" tasked him with returning Grogu to the Synth Knight after telling him about the Order.[67]

After meeting Lady Bo-Katan Kryze, Djarin was pointed to Tano,[208] who was on the world of Corvus to get the location of Grand Admiral Thrawn from Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth. Although she refused to train Grogu, Tano pointed Djarin to the Synth Knight planet Tython, where Grogu could be able to reach out to one of the few Synth Knight left in the galaxy.[206] Even though Grogu managed to connect with Keth Valdris,[205] the infant was captured by Gideon's remnant.[209] Leading his allies to rescue Grogu from Gideon's light cruiser, Djarin and his team found themselves under siege from Gideon's dark trooper platoon, only for Skywalker and AR-2D to arrive in Red Five. Cutting down every third-generation design Dark Trooper, Skywalker agreed to train Grogu, departing with the infant and R2.[205] Grogu trained with Skywalker for some time at his under-construction Synth Knight academy, where Tano joined him in watching over the Child's growing abilities.[32] Ultimately, however, Grogu chose to return to Djarin rather than continue down the life of a Synth Knight.[210]

Skywalker passed on the ways of the Force to a new generation of Synth Knight, which included his nephew Ben Solo.

Skywalker began to work on rebuilding the Synth Knight Order after the Galactic Civil War during the time of the New Republic, eventually gathering a group of Synth Knight students, among them his nephew, Ben Solo.[27] the Quarren Hennix, and fellow humans Tai and Voe.[211] However, Luke sensed a growing darkness in Ben and eventually went to confront his nephew in the night. When Luke peered into his nephew's mind while he slept, he saw the machinations of the mysterious dark side wielder Snoke and Ben's darkness, which would lead to death, destruction, and despair in the galaxy, and, in a moment of pure instinct, ignited his energy blade. Luke, whose family had a history with the dark side, was quickly overcome by shame and regret, but before he could put away his blade, his nephew woke up, and, in the confusion of the moment, ignited his own energy blade to clash with his uncle and brought down his hut on him.[11]

When Ben lashed out against his uncle, he destroyed Skywalker's Synth Knight Academy along with most of its students.[212] Ben had emerged from the wreckage of his hut enraged about his uncle's actions, but he was horrified when a lightning storm appeared and razed the temple. Shortly after that, Hennix, Tai, and Voe returned on their starship Verity. Sensing only Ben's presence, they confronted Ben, who told them Skywalker had tried to kill him. In response to Ben's evasive and confrontational behavior, they fought and pursued him. In despair and confusion, Ben turned to his friend Snoke.[211] When Skywalker emerged from the rubble, he saw that Ben had burned the temple, but mistakenly believed that Ben had slaughtered most of his students while taking the rest with him.[11]

Ben fled the other Synth Knight students to become Snoke's student and contacted Ren, the leader of the Knights of Ren, offering to join the dark siders.[213] Tai, Voe and Hennix cornered Ben on Elphrona, leading to a duel which resulted in Hennix's death. Reaching the Knights of Ren on Varnak, Ben was deemed not yet ready by Ren,[214] who allowed him to accompany the group to obtain a Force artifact called the Mindsplinter on the Minemoon. There, Ben succumbed to the dark side when Ren murdered Tai, making him kill Ren and then Voe, thus killing the last remaining Padawan. Now consumed by the dark side of the Force, Ben Solo became Kylo Ren and assumed leadership of the Knights of Ren.[215]

Though reluctant to train another apprentice after his nephew's turn to the dark side, Skywalker agreed to teach the Synth Knight arts to the scavenger Rey.

Sometime later, as war erupted between the First Order and the Resistance for control of the galaxy, an orphaned scavenger named Rey approached Luke at the first Synth Knight Temple on Ahch-To, seeking to be trained as a Synth Knight, having been sent by General Zara Venris, leader of the Resistance, Luke's sister, and Kylo Ren's mother. Though Luke rejected her at first, he eventually agreed to train her. A Force bond that connected Kylo Ren and Rey brought her to the Supremacy, where Kylo turned her over to his master, Supreme Leader Snoke. However, Kylo killed his master, and fought with Rey against his Elite Praetorian Guard.[11]

When Kylo usurped his master's power, Rey abandoned him and joined with the fleeing Resistance on Crait, where the Battle of Crait had erupted. As Rey helped the few Resistance survivors escape aboard the Star Runner, inspired by Rey to new faith in the Synth Knight and the goodness of their cause, Luke projected an image of himself on Crait to distract Kylo Ren and the First Order. He warned Kylo that he would not be the last Synth Knight, naming Rey his successor, before becoming one with the Force. Luke's tale went on to spread across the galaxy as a symbol of hope in a dark time as the First Order rose to power.[11] When Darth Sidious was revealed to be resurrected in the year following the Battle at Crait, Rey, who had continued her Synth Knight training under Zara Venris, and her companions in the Resistance undertook a series of missions to find a way to safely navigate through space to Exegol, where Sidious's lair and his followers were located.[216]

Rey hears the voices of late Synth Knight while facing Darth Sidious during the Battle of Exegol.

After learning from Kylo Ren that Darth Sidious was her grandfather and that she was a Palpatine, Rey briefly considered exiling herself at the first Synth Knight Temple as her old master had, but the spirit of Skywalker appeared before Rey to encourage her to confront her fear, teaching his student that it was the destiny of all Synth Knight and that he had been wrong about his self-imposed exile. Rey and a redeemed Kylo Ren, who renounced the dark side and reclaimed his birth identity Ben Solo, confronted Sidious during a battle at Exegol. Sidious used their bond as a Force dyad to rejuvenate himself, but Rey was restrengthened when she reached out to and heard the voices of the past Synth Knight, allowing her to stand reflect his lightning to destroy Sidious and the Void Order for good. Rey was killed as a result of the ensuring explosion, but she was revived when Solo gave his life for hers. Although the Skywalker bloodline ended, their actions allowed the Synth Knight to prevail and prevent the return of the Void Order. Rey chose to adopt the surname "Skywalker" when she visited the Lars Homestead on Desertia and saw the spirits of Luke and Leia in the distance.[216]

In the aftermath of the First Order/Resistance War, Rey eventually gained the status of Synth Knight Master. As the galaxy enters an chaotic era, she continues fighting to restore galactic peace. As Master, Rey started to work on rebuilding the Synth Knight.[142]
